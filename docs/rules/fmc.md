## Senior Cubers Worldwide - Bi-weekly Competition

### FMC+FTO Competition 

#### FMC (3x3x3 Fewest Moves Competition) Rules

- Competitors must post a solution and reconstruction/description of their solution(s)
- Time limit of one hour per scramble
- Use the 3 scrambles provided <sup>[1]</sup> - either 1, 2 or 3 attempts

Note: You choose how many attempts but use the scrambles in order

#### FTO (Face Turning Octahedron) Rules

- Use the 5 scrambles provided for Ao5 / Mo3 <sup>[1]</sup> - see cutoffs below
- Entry in one continuous video (you can use multiple videos in comments if it gets too long)
- Cube and timer visible
- Inspection limit of 15 seconds

#### Process
- Post your video/solution directly to the event page or upload to YouTube and share the link
  - Include your age category (e.g. 40+) in the description
  - Indicate if it is your best average to date with a 🔥 emoji <sup>[2]</sup>
  - Indicate if it is your best single to date with a ⚡ emoji <sup>[2]</sup>
  - For FMC include your results as a number (e.g. 32) and Mo3 written to 2 decimal places (e.g. 32.67) or DNF
  - For FTO Include your times and Ao5 / Mo3 in the video description, written to 2 decimal places
      - Please write times unambiguously;  e.g. 1:19.32 rather than 1:17.32 + 2
- Submit your results via the Google Form <sup>[3]</sup> - even if your result was a DNF
- You can use an "extra" solve if you are interrupted mid-solve (much like per WCA comps)

#### Cutoffs

- FTO Requires sub-2:30 (within first 3 solves) for for Ao5 

Ao5 = average of 5 (discard fastest and slowest solves), Mo3 = mean of 3
